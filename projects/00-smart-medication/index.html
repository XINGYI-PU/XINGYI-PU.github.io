Smart Medication Management System

An ongoing IoT project exploring a Bluetooth-enabled pillbox concept, medication reminders, and a WeChat Mini Program.

January 2026-Present · Personal project

Overview

I am developing a smart medication management system that combines Internet of Things (IoT) technology with medication reminders and digital tracking. The project explores how connected devices and a mobile interface can support medication management and user interaction.

Current work

  • Designed a Bluetooth-enabled pillbox concept for medication management.
  • Developing a WeChat Mini Program for reminders and user interaction.
  • Exploring medication adherence tracking and smart healthcare applications.
  • Gaining hands-on experience in IoT, product design, and problem solving.

The hardware is at the concept stage and the Mini Program is in development.

Technologies and skills: Internet of Things (IoT), WeChat Mini Programs, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and product design.
