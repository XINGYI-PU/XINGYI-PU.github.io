## Overview

AI Pillbox is a lightweight coursework and research prototype with separate patient and doctor portals. It is structured as an X/Y/Z experimental platform for studying how different levels of digital intervention may affect medication adherence. It is not a medical device and is not intended for clinical use.

## Main features

- A patient portal for recording medication, suggested intake time, study group, and history.
- A doctor portal for viewing records, summary statistics, patient timelines, and time-based suggestions.
- Three study conditions: intelligent intervention, basic reminders, and usual-care control.
- Local browser storage combined with a Google Sheets data store.
- A Google Apps Script HTTP interface for inserting and reading study records.
- A structure suitable for later adherence-rate comparisons and statistical analysis across groups.

**Technologies:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Axios, Google Apps Script, and Google Sheets.

Source code is currently unavailable publicly.
